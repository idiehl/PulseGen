LGPL COMPONENT MODIFICATION AND DEBUGGING

PulseGen distributes libsndfile, LAME and mpg123 under their upstream LGPL
licenses. Their license texts, copyright notices, source locations and source
archive hashes accompany this distribution. A companion third-party source
archive is available alongside the Windows download in the same release.

Recipients may modify or replace those LGPL components and reverse engineer
the combined application for debugging modifications to those components, as
provided by their LGPL licenses. No PulseGen distribution term restricts these
activities. This permission does not select a general open-source license for
PulseGen's own code or change the licenses of any other component.

The normal codec load verifies the supplied sndfile.dll by SHA-256. The explicit
custom-codec workflow permits use of a compatible modified library while making
that change visible. Such a library may alter output, reliability, security or
render reproducibility; its identity must be recorded separately from the
original checksum-pinned build.
